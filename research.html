Research

Experimental development and materials physics under dynamic extreme conditions.

Fe–Si alloys at super-Earth core conditions

Laser-driven ramp compression combined with in-situ x-ray diffraction to investigate phase stability of Fe–Si alloys at hundreds of gigapascals to terapascal pressures.

Re-based high-entropy alloys

Composition-dependent structural behavior of Re-rich and Re-poor HEAs during shock compression and release, using x-ray diffraction and velocimetry.

Phase transitions and kinetics in NaCl

Ultrafast x-ray diffraction at MEC/LCLS coupled with velocimetry to resolve B1→B2 transformation, melting and release kinetics on nanosecond timescales.

High-repetition-rate XFEL target development

Design and experimental validation of slurry targets and target architectures for rapid replenishment in future high-repetition-rate laser/XFEL experiments.

Time-resolved Raman spectroscopy

Development of an in-situ time-resolved Raman facility and application to polymers and dynamically shocked materials.

Laser-plasma ion diagnostics

Development of a high-resolution Thomson parabola spectrometer for in-situ characterization of ion parameters from laser-produced ion sources.
