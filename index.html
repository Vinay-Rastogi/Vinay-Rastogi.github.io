01 — ABOUT

Research profile

I am an experimental physicist working in high energy density science, laser-driven shock compression, dynamic materials, and time-resolved X-ray/optical diagnostics. My research combines advanced experimental platforms with quantitative analysis to study phase transitions, structural dynamics, and material response under extreme conditions.

My work spans dynamic compression, high-pressure materials, high-entropy alloys, time-resolved X-ray diffraction, VISAR/velocimetry, Raman spectroscopy, laser–matter interaction, target development, and scientific data analysis.

02 — RESEARCH

Research areas

01

Dynamic Compression

Shock and ramp compression experiments to probe materials under extreme pressure and strain rates.

02

High-Pressure Materials

Phase stability, structural transformations, equations of state, and behavior of condensed matter at extreme conditions.

03

High-Entropy Alloys

Structural evolution, phase stability, and metastable behavior of multicomponent alloys under dynamic loading.

04

Time-Resolved XRD

In-situ diffraction measurements combined with VISAR and other diagnostics to resolve transient structures.

05

DAC & Raman

Static compression and laser-heating studies using diamond-anvil cells and Raman spectroscopy.

06

Laser–Matter Interaction

High-power laser experiments, target development, laser-driven compression, and transient diagnostics.

Dynamic compression of Re-based high-entropy alloys

FE–SI

RAMP COMPRESSION · TPa · XRD

Fe–Si alloys at terapascal pressures

Studying phase stability and structural behavior of Fe–Si alloys under laser-driven ramp compression, with implications for planetary interiors.

DAC · RAMAN · XRD

Exploring structural and vibrational behavior of nanocrystalline materials under static compression and laser heating.
